# OBJECTIVE TYPE QUESTIONS

## M001

**प्रश्न / Question:** यूक्लिड विभाजन प्रमेयिका a = bq + r (b > 0) में शेषफल के लिए सही शर्त क्या है?

*In Euclid’s division lemma a = bq + r (b > 0), the remainder satisfies:*

**Correct option: (A) — 0 ≤ r < b**

**व्याख्या / Worked explanation:**

Euclid’s division lemma is a = bq + r with 0 ≤ r < b for b > 0. The remainder cannot be negative or at least as large as the divisor.

## M002

**प्रश्न / Question:** 78 को 7 से भाग देने पर शेषफल क्या होगा?

*What is the remainder when 78 is divided by 7?*

**Correct option: (B) — 1**

**व्याख्या / Worked explanation:**

78 = 7 × 11 + 1. The remainder after division by 7 is therefore 1.

## M003

**प्रश्न / Question:** 36 और 48 का महत्तम समापवर्तक (म.स.) क्या है?

*The HCF of 36 and 48 is:*

**Correct option: (C) — 12**

**व्याख्या / Worked explanation:**

36 = 2² × 3² and 48 = 2⁴ × 3. Take the lowest common powers: HCF = 2² × 3 = 12.

## M004

**प्रश्न / Question:** 12 और 18 का लघुत्तम समापवर्त्य (ल.स.) क्या है?

*The LCM of 12 and 18 is:*

**Correct option: (C) — 36**

**व्याख्या / Worked explanation:**

12 = 2² × 3 and 18 = 2 × 3². Take the highest prime powers: LCM = 2² × 3² = 36.

## M005

**प्रश्न / Question:** धनात्मक पूर्णांकों a और b के लिए म.स. × ल.स. का मान किसके बराबर होता है?

*For positive integers a and b, HCF(a,b) × LCM(a,b) equals:*

**Correct option: (B) — a × b**

**व्याख्या / Worked explanation:**

For two positive integers, their product equals HCF × LCM. Thus HCF(a,b) × LCM(a,b) = ab.

## L001

**प्रश्न / Question:** सिद्ध कीजिए कि √5 एक अपरिमेय संख्या है। अतः सिद्ध कीजिए कि 3 + 2√5 अपरिमेय है।

*Prove that √5 is irrational. Hence prove that 3 + 2√5 is irrational.*

**हल / Model answer:**

**Step 1 (contradiction):** Suppose √5 = p/q in lowest terms with integers p, q and q ≠ 0. Squaring, p² = 5q². Hence 5 divides p², so 5 divides p; put p = 5k. Then 25k² = 5q², so q² = 5k² and 5 divides q. This contradicts gcd(p,q) = 1. Thus √5 is irrational.

**Step 2:** If 3 + 2√5 were rational (say r), then √5 = (r − 3)/2 would be rational because rational numbers are closed under subtraction and division by 2. Contradiction. Therefore **3 + 2√5 is irrational**.

## L003

**प्रश्न / Question:** समकोण त्रिभुज में पाइथागोरस प्रमेय से (जहाँ परिभाषित हों) ये तीनों सर्वसमिकाएँ सिद्ध कीजिए: (i) sin²θ + cos²θ = 1; (ii) 1 + tan²θ = sec²θ; (iii) 1 + cot²θ = cosec²θ।

*Starting from the Pythagoras theorem in a right triangle, derive these three identities (where defined): (i) sin²θ + cos²θ = 1; (ii) 1 + tan²θ = sec²θ; (iii) 1 + cot²θ = cosec²θ.*

**हल / Model answer:**

Let O = opposite, A = adjacent and H = hypotenuse of a right triangle. Pythagoras gives O² + A² = H².

(i) Divide by H²: (O/H)² + (A/H)² = 1, i.e. **sin²θ + cos²θ = 1**.

(ii) Divide by A²: (O/A)² + 1 = (H/A)², giving **1 + tan²θ = sec²θ**.

(iii) Divide by O²: 1 + (A/O)² = (H/O)², giving **1 + cot²θ = cosec²θ**. All identities hold wherever their ratios are defined.

## L004

**प्रश्न / Question:** न्यून कोण θ के लिए सिद्ध कीजिए: (1 + sin θ)/cos θ + cos θ/(1 + sin θ) = 2 sec θ।

*For an acute angle θ, prove: (1 + sin θ)/cos θ + cos θ/(1 + sin θ) = 2 sec θ.*

**हल / Model answer:**

Start from the left side L = (1 + sinθ)/cosθ + cosθ/(1 + sinθ). Multiply the second fraction by (1 − sinθ)/(1 − sinθ):

cosθ/(1 + sinθ) = cosθ(1 − sinθ)/(1 − sin²θ) = cosθ(1 − sinθ)/cos²θ = (1 − sinθ)/cosθ.

Therefore L = [(1 + sinθ) + (1 − sinθ)]/cosθ = 2/cosθ = **2 secθ**, which equals the right side.
